# Fresh SignBridge retest — 3 October 2026

Requested after the initial implementation report. Testing used fresh isolated browser participants at `127.0.0.1:5174` and the local room server at port 3001. The user's existing room, camera and saved Sign/Text preferences were preserved.

## Two issues found and fixed

1. **Input provenance:** with Sign selected, fresh keyboard text was incorrectly sent as “Reviewed sign.” Fresh manual text now sends “Typed,” without sign-language metadata. Editing reviewed speech/sign drafts retains their captured source and sign language; uncertain retries retain the original metadata and ID. Three regression tests cover typing under Sign preferences, captured ASL through preference changes/retry, and dictation replacing a sign draft.
2. **Pending microphone feedback:** a pending browser microphone request had no visible starting state or cancellation control. The hook now tracks `off / starting / on / error`. The conversation shows waiting-permission guidance and **Cancel microphone start**; typing stays usable. Cancellation invalidates pending acquisition and stops any late stream. Three further regressions cover pending/cancel/late acquisition, permission-error preservation and the conversation controls.
