# Conversation workflow pilot

Status: protocol prepared; no participants recruited and no real-user outcomes collected by this implementation. Use the blank [run sheet](user-pilot-run-sheet.csv). Automated and same-laptop browser results belong in the separate engineering report.

## Consent and setup

Recruit two consenting pairs for formative feedback, then aim for five pairs for the comparison. Explain the experiment, voluntary participation, the right to stop and what will be retained. Use anonymous pair codes; do not record call video/audio by default. Ask each person to choose their own input and output. Product screens do not assign disability categories.

Use the tested Windows Chrome/Android Chrome setup and document browser versions, actual devices and network types. Support each person's preferred accessible output. Fluent ISL/ASL reviewers are needed for any evaluation involving those languages; report them separately. A mock camera, browser-tab pair or typed sign label is not a signing-language user trial.

## Formative experiment

With two pairs, compare ordinary conversation and a lightweight walkthrough of the new controls:

1. Clarify which entrance a sender meant.
2. Change a meeting time after a first agreement.
3. Distinguish two forms using different labels and descriptions.

Ask whether optional controls helped, where they interrupted conversation and what was still unclear. If a control adds effort without useful benefit, simplify it before the larger trial. Do not infer understanding from clicking a button.

## Matched comparison

Each of five pairs attempts five baseline tasks with ordinary chat/video and five matched tasks with optional clarification/cards/references: 50 attempts total, 25 per condition. Ordinary participants may ask clarifying questions using their normal messages. Do not artificially prevent that in the baseline.

Alternate condition order across pairs. Use distinct but comparable task details rather than repeating an already learned answer. Document order and any practice. Give the sender a private task sheet; ask the receiver to state final details independently. Compare to the sheet, not to the state of an approval checkbox. Use plain campus situations rather than clinical/legal decisions.

| Task family | Baseline example | Feature-condition example | Check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1: Ambiguous location | Meet at the library entrance; intended side is beside the main road. | Meet at the auditorium entrance; intended side is beside the garden. | Receiver identifies the intended entrance. |
| 2: Changed time | Agree on 15:00, then change to 16:00 on the same explicit date/time zone. | Approve 14:00, then propose 15:00 on a different explicit date/time zone. | Receiver states the latest time, and prior agreement is not mistaken for approval of the change. |
| 3: Similar references | Two forms named Application A/B with different descriptions. | Two forms named Registration A/B with different descriptions. | Receiver chooses the intended form from its description. |
| 4: Sender correction | Sender corrects the initially sent room from 201 to 210. | Sender corrects the initially sent room from 301 to 310. | Receiver states the corrected room; original wording remains inspectable. |
| 5: Multiple details | Arrange a study meeting with date, time, time zone and place. | Arrange a project meeting with equivalent fields and a short note. | Every required final field matches the sheet. |

Set the actual dates/time zones before a session and give both conditions equally clear instructions. If shared references are not included in a tested build, report task 3 separately or revise the protocol before collecting results.

## Recording and interpretation

Record completion, final-detail correctness, elapsed time, clarification attempts, incorrect details approved/claimed agreed, chosen modes, order, issues and preference. Record interruptions separately. Include all attempts and explain exclusions; do not drop failures to improve the result.

Targets are at least 23/25 correct feature-condition tasks, zero observed incorrect final details marked approved in this sample, and at least three of five pairs preferring optional controls for these tasks. Report baseline results and added effort beside those targets. If the feature flow performs worse or is burdensome, change it rather than claim success.

These targets are not achieved results. The small pilot does not establish general effectiveness or statistical significance. Repeat attempts from the same pair are not independent participants. If five pairs, assistive technology or either sign language are unavailable, state the actual scope and leave the corresponding validation incomplete.

## Demo and privacy

A live demonstration may use a separate test room and synthetic meeting details. Label recordings as recordings and scripted demonstrations as scripted. Publish aggregate results only with permission; keep invite URLs, credentials, personal conversations and participant identity out of screenshots/reports. Saving a correction does not consent to saving training data or footage.
