## Contribution being implemented

SignBridge extends its existing two-person conversation with optional clarification linked to a message, immutable linked corrections, meeting details approved only for their exact revision, and text reference descriptions captured with a sent message. The ordinary reviewed Send flow remains available. Device delivery, a person's resolved request and both participants' approvals are distinct states.

This is a differentiation hypothesis about the integrated workflow. The sources above do not prove its global novelty. The useful claim requires the planned [pilot](user-pilot-protocol-2026-10-03.md) and observed outcomes; implementing a feature does not demonstrate reduced misunderstandings.

## Claims to use in a demonstration

- After verification: “We implemented optional message clarification, linked corrections and revision-specific meeting approvals in one conversation screen.”
- After actual user evaluation: state the participant/task counts, conditions and observed differences, including failures.
- Recognition remains isolated-word research. ISL and ASL are separate; a live video call does not translate between them. Saved word clips in text order are not fluent sentence translation.
- Do not say “world first,” guaranteed understanding, validated universal accessibility, or improved signing accuracy based on workflow tests.
